# Hero Image Generation Prompts

These are the prompts used to generate the portfolio hero images.

- Normal portrait: `1-withoutDigital.png`
- Digital interaction portrait: `2-1-withDigital.png`

## Prompt 1: Cinematic Portrait Without Digital Effects

Create a premium, ultra-realistic cinematic portrait photograph inspired by the provided reference image.

**Subject & Composition:**

- A young professional with a confident, intelligent, and approachable appearance.
- Position the subject on the **right side of the image**, occupying approximately 40% of the frame.
- The subject should be **looking toward the left side** with a natural, thoughtful expression.
- Slightly turn the upper body toward the left.
- Maintain a natural, relaxed posture with subtle hand gestures.
- Preserve realistic facial features, natural skin texture, and accurate body proportions.
- Wear a minimalist black or deep navy outfit.

**Color Palette — Midnight Sapphire:**

- #0B1026 — Deep midnight background
- #172554 — Rich sapphire shadows
- #3556A8 — Primary blue illumination
- #A7BDF2 — Soft lavender-blue highlights
- #F1F5FF — Subtle icy-white accents

**Lighting & Atmosphere:**

- Professional cinematic studio lighting.
- Soft sapphire-blue key light illuminating the face from the left.
- Gentle lavender-blue rim lighting outlining the hair and shoulders.
- Subtle icy-white highlights for facial definition.
- Deep midnight-blue shadows for a premium, sophisticated appearance.
- Smooth gradient background transitioning from #0B1026 to #172554 with subtle #3556A8 illumination.
- Elegant, modern technology-inspired atmosphere without excessive neon effects.

**Background & Layout:**

- Clean, minimal, seamless studio background.
- Keep the **left 60% of the image empty**, providing negative space for future text or graphic elements.
- No distracting objects, patterns, or unnecessary visual effects.
- Smooth cinematic gradients and subtle atmospheric depth.

**Photography Quality:**

- Ultra-realistic professional portrait photography.
- High-end editorial and personal branding aesthetic.
- Sharp facial details, natural skin tones, controlled contrast.
- Soft cinematic shadows and realistic light falloff.
- Premium color grading using only the Midnight Sapphire palette.
- 16:9 widescreen composition, suitable for a LinkedIn banner or professional technology portfolio.

**Avoid:** Green lighting, teal backgrounds, warm orange tones, excessive neon glow, artificial-looking skin, distorted hands, text, logos, watermarks, and cluttered backgrounds.

## Prompt 2: Cinematic Portrait With Digital Interaction

Create a premium, ultra-realistic cinematic technology portrait using the uploaded reference photographs of the young woman. Preserve her facial identity, facial structure, hairstyle, and natural appearance accurately.

**Subject & Composition:**

- Position the woman on the right side, occupying approximately 40–45% of the image.
- She is looking toward the left, interacting with an invisible futuristic digital interface.
- Her right arm extends naturally toward the left, with fingers slightly separated as though touching a floating holographic screen.
- Her other hand rests naturally near her waist or lightly interacts with a second digital control.
- Give her a confident, intelligent, curious expression with a subtle natural smile.
- Wear an elegant, minimalist black or midnight-navy long-sleeved outfit.
- Maintain natural body proportions, realistic hands, and authentic facial details.

**Digital Interaction Concept:**

- Create the impression that she is interacting with advanced AI technology through spatial computing.
- Include extremely subtle, transparent holographic interface elements near her fingertips.
- Add delicate sapphire-blue light particles and thin digital connection lines responding to her hand movements.
- Suggest artificial intelligence, digital innovation, and human–computer interaction.
- Make the interaction feel physically realistic, as though she is controlling a sophisticated invisible digital environment.
- Do not use a VR headset. Keep her face clearly visible and recognizable.
- Avoid oversized holographic screens, excessive floating icons, or distracting interface graphics.

**Midnight Sapphire Color Palette:**

- #0B1026 — Deep midnight background
- #172554 — Rich sapphire shadows
- #3556A8 — Digital interaction illumination
- #A7BDF2 — Lavender-blue edge lighting
- #F1F5FF — Icy-white holographic highlights

**Lighting & Atmosphere:**

- Cinematic sapphire-blue studio lighting from the left.
- Soft lavender-blue rim lighting around the hair and shoulders.
- Subtle reflections from the digital interface illuminating her fingertips and face.
- Deep midnight-blue shadows with smooth gradients.
- Elegant, futuristic, sophisticated technology atmosphere.
- Preserve natural skin tones without excessive blue color casts.

**Background & Layout:**

- Seamless gradient background transitioning between #0B1026, #172554, and #3556A8.
- Keep approximately 55–60% of the left side visually clean and spacious for future website text.
- Concentrate subtle holographic effects around her extended hand.
- No distracting objects or cluttered backgrounds.
- Maintain the same cinematic framing and directional interaction as the uploaded VR reference photograph.

**Photography Quality:**

- Ultra-realistic professional studio photography.
- High-end technology editorial aesthetic.
- Realistic skin texture, detailed hair, and accurate hand anatomy.
- Natural cinematic depth, controlled contrast, and premium color grading.
- 16:9 widescreen landscape composition.
- Suitable for a professional personal portfolio, technology website hero section, or LinkedIn cover design.

**Avoid:** VR headsets, green or teal lighting, orange tones, cartoon effects, excessive neon, distorted fingers, unrealistic facial features, generic AI-generated faces, text, logos, watermarks, and cluttered digital overlays.
